# Repository guidelines

## Commit messages

Do not add AI attribution to commits in this repository. That means:

- no "Generated with …" or "Built with …" footer lines,
- no `Co-Authored-By` trailers for an AI tool or assistant,
- no robot emoji or similar attribution footers.

End the commit message on its own last line. A normal one-line subject plus an
optional short body describing why the change was made is all that is wanted.
